Heal Together in Nature

Join our therapeutic group trips designed to promote healing, connection, and wellness through nature-based activities and community support.

Upcoming Group Trips

March 15-18, 2024

Mountain Wellness Retreat

Location: Manali, Himachal Pradesh

Duration: 4 days, 3 nights

Group Size: Maximum 12 participants

Wellness Activities:

  • Morning meditation and yoga sessions
  • Guided nature walks and hiking
  • Group therapy sessions in outdoor settings
  • Mindfulness workshops
  • Evening reflection circles around campfire
  • Healthy meals and nutrition workshops
Safety Notes: All activities are optional and can be adapted to individual comfort levels. Medical support will be available throughout the trip. Participants should be in reasonably good physical health.
April 20-23, 2024

Coastal Healing Journey

Location: Goa

Duration: 4 days, 3 nights

Group Size: Maximum 12 participants

Wellness Activities:

  • Beach meditation and sunrise sessions
  • Water-based activities (swimming, kayaking)
  • Group discussions on the beach
  • Art therapy and journaling workshops
  • Evening beach walks and stargazing
  • Healthy local cuisine experiences
Safety Notes: Swimming activities are optional and supervised. All participants should inform facilitators of any water-related concerns. Life jackets will be provided for water activities.
May 10-13, 2024

Forest Mindfulness Retreat

Location: Coorg, Karnataka

Duration: 4 days, 3 nights

Group Size: Maximum 12 participants

Wellness Activities:

  • Forest bathing and nature immersion
  • Guided meditation in natural settings
  • Group therapy in outdoor pavilions
  • Wildlife observation and bird watching
  • Evening storytelling and sharing circles
  • Organic farm-to-table meals
Safety Notes: All activities are conducted with respect for the natural environment. Participants should bring appropriate clothing for forest conditions. Medical support and emergency protocols are in place.
June 5-8, 2024

Desert Wellness Experience

Location: Jaisalmer, Rajasthan

Duration: 4 days, 3 nights

Group Size: Maximum 12 participants

Wellness Activities:

  • Desert camping and stargazing
  • Sunrise and sunset meditation
  • Group therapy under open skies
  • Camel rides and desert exploration
  • Cultural immersion experiences
  • Evening music and dance therapy
Safety Notes: Desert conditions require appropriate hydration and sun protection. All participants will receive safety briefings. Comfortable accommodations with climate control are provided.
